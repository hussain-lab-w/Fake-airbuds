# Premium music app patterns for EW27 Player

Research synthesized from Spotify, Apple Music, TIDAL, YouTube Music, Marvis Pro, and Doppler.

## Direction
EW27 should feel like a beautiful, dependable instrument for owned music: one playback spine, one persistent mini-player, one expanded Now Playing surface, structured queue, local synchronized lyrics, clear Audio Profiles, artwork-led adaptive theming, and curated local library shelves.

## Priorities
1. Single playback spine: persistent mini-player -> expanded Now Playing; no duplicate bars.
2. Queue workspace: Now Playing / Up Next / Added by You, Play Next/Add to End, reorder, swipe remove, clear, persist.
3. Lyrics mode: compact optional preview, full reader, current-line emphasis, tap-to-seek, manual scroll pause, explicit unavailable state.
4. Audio Profiles: named presets, custom EQ, bypass, headroom, factual format/output badges.
5. Curated local library: Jump Back In, Recently Added, Favorites, Recently Played, Albums, Artists, Playlists.
6. Artwork-led theming with contrast-safe accent and restrained motion.
7. Playback continuity: prebuffer, gapless/crossfade, session restoration, route-aware behavior.
