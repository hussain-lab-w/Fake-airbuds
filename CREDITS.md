## Adapted from Beans Music

Some Liquid Glass rendering patterns in `EW27Player/UI/VisualPolish.swift` were
adapted from **Beans Music** (https://github.com/XIaodou0416/Beans-Music), which
is distributed under the MIT License:

- the specular edge highlight drawn over Apple's `glassEffect`, which is what
  makes a pane read as glass rather than a flat translucent rectangle
- wrapping surfaces in `GlassEffectContainer`
- the morphing play/pause icon built from two shapes

No network, API or playback code was taken from that project; EW27 Player keeps
its own `AVAudioEngine` graph and local-library playback.
